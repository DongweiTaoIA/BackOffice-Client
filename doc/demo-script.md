# BackOffice Admin Portal — Demo Script

> Innovation pitch · business-focused talk track
> Target length: ~10 minutes  ·  Audience: business stakeholders

---

## Slide 1 — Title  ·  *30 sec*

> "Hi everyone — thanks for joining.
> Today I want to walk you through an innovation idea we've been building:
> **the BackOffice Admin Portal.**
> The idea is simple — give our admin team **one secure, dedicated portal** to do their job, with **self-service tools** for everything they used to have to ask IT for."

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem Today  ·  *60 sec*

> "Right now our admins work out of **Unifi** — a **very old** system — to manage **contracts, claims, statements and dealers**.
> Three things are hurting us:
> **1. It's shared.** The *same* Unifi UI is used by external users. Mixing admin functions with external access is a real security concern.
> **2. No self-service.** Unifi has no proper screens to **set up a program, set up an operator, or fix data**. Every time an admin needs one of those, they file an **IT ticket** — and wait days for someone on our team to run a script.
> **3. It's not alone.** Around Unifi we also have **Unification, TPA, Batch Jobs, Payment Processing** — each with its own login and UI. Admins spend more time **jumping between systems** than doing the actual work."

---

## Slide 3 — The Vision  ·  *60 sec*

> "Our vision is straightforward: **one dedicated, admin-only portal.**
> Three things change:
> **Secure** — admin functions live in their *own* app, separate from anything external users touch.
> **Self-service** — program setup, operator setup, role management, data uploads and fixes become **clicks**, not IT tickets.
> **Unified** — Unifi, Unification, TPA, Batch Jobs and Payments are all reachable from the same sidebar. The portal is the **single front door** for everything admin."

---

## Slide 4 — Why Now  ·  *45 sec*

> "Three things make this the right moment:
> **Efficiency** — collapse multi-system workflows and kill the IT-ticket queue.
> **Security** — finally separate admin tooling from external-facing systems.
> **AI** — large language models are good enough now to act as a real co-pilot inside the portal."

---

## Slide 5 — What is the BackOffice Admin Portal  ·  *45 sec*

> "It's a modern, **admin-only** web console.
> Admins sign in with their **Microsoft account**, land on a **live dashboard** that aggregates data across every connected system, and from there they can **search, drill in, set things up, and take action** — all in one screen.
> No installs. Works on any laptop. Always up to date."

---

## Slide 6 — A Better Way to Work  ·  *60 sec*

> "Here's what changes for the admin team.
> The system they use today is **slow, clunky and aging** — every routine task feels like a chore, and anything beyond the basics turns into an IT ticket.
> The new portal gives them a **better way to do the same job**: a clean, modern UI built around how admins actually work, with **search, drill-in and action all in one place**.
> Most importantly, the **routine work that gets handed off to IT today — program setup, operator setup, role changes, data uploads, data fixes — moves into the admins' own hands**.
> Less waiting. Less back-and-forth. More work getting done."

*(We'll frame this as a qualitative shift; hard numbers will follow once the portal is in real use.)*

---

## Slide 7 — Before vs After  ·  *45 sec*

> "Same workflow, two worlds.
> **Before:** old Unifi UI shared with external users, plus separate systems for Unification, TPA, Batch Jobs and Payments — each with its own login and UI — and an IT ticket every time a program or operator needs to be set up.
> **After:** one admin-only portal, **self-service** setup screens, AI does the lookup across every connected system, every action is logged automatically."

---

## Slide 8 — Live Dashboard  ·  *45 sec*

> "The home screen gives admins a **real-time pulse** across every connected system.
> Today we show six live counters from Unifi — **dealers, products, programs, contracts, customers, vehicles** — plus a recent-activity feed that updates as new contracts and customers come in.
> Unification sits beside it as a preview lane, and **TPA, Batch Jobs and Payments** will plug into the same pattern as we wire them up."

---

## Slide 9 — Manage the Whole Ecosystem  ·  *60 sec*

> "From one sidebar, admins reach every back-office capability — today and as we grow.
> **Live now:** Dealer Servicing — dealers, contracts, claims, cancellations.
> **Coming next:** statements; **TPA**, **Batch Jobs** and **Payments** modules; and the self-service admin tools — **Program Setup**, **Operator Setup**, **Roles & Permissions**, **Data Tools** (bulk upload, data fix, exports), plus **Reports & Audit** and **Configuration**.
> Every module follows the same pattern — search, filter, drill in, act — so once an admin learns one, they know them all."

---

## Slide 10 — AI Assistant Built In  ·  *60 sec*

> "The killer feature is the **AI panel** sitting on the right of every screen.
> **Today** admins can ask things like *"find dealer 12345"* or *"can this contract be cancelled?"* — and the assistant answers, opens the right page, or kicks off the workflow.
> **As we add more modules**, the same chat will handle *"why did last night's batch fail?"*, *"show me payments stuck in retry"*, *"set up a new program based on this template"*.
> No more remembering which system owns which screen — and no more waiting on IT for routine setup."

---

## Slide 11 — Voice of the User  ·  *45 sec*

> "We also built the **feedback loop into the product itself**.
> Right from the sidebar, any admin can **report a bug, request a feature, or ask for help** — with rich text, screenshots, the works.
> Every piece of feedback flows straight into our backlog. We listen, we ship, we improve."

---

## Slide 12 — Impact Snapshot  ·  *45 sec*

> "If we hit our targets, the win is concrete:
> fewer security risks, **far fewer IT tickets**, faster onboarding for new admins, fewer mistakes, less system-hopping, happier admins, and **one platform we can keep growing on** as new back-office systems come online."

---

## Slide 13 — Roadmap  ·  *45 sec*

> "**Now:** unified dashboard, dealer servicing (dealers, contracts, claims, cancellations), AI chat, in-product feedback.
> **Next:** self-service admin — Program Setup, Operator Setup, Roles & Permissions, Data Tools; plus TPA, Batch Jobs and Payments modules.
> **Later:** full Unification cut-over, deeper cross-system AI workflows, in-app docs."

---

## Slide 14 — Live Demo  ·  *3 min*

> "Let me show you the real thing."
> 1. Sign in.
> 2. Dashboard — point out live counters across systems + activity feed.
> 3. Dealers → List → Map → drill into one.
> 4. Contracts → open one → start a Cancellation → recalc refund.
> 5. AI chat — *"find dealers in Quebec"* → results open in main pane.
> 6. Sidebar → **Request a Feature** → show the rich-text dialog and the submission flow.

---

## Slide 15 — Thank You / Q&A  ·  *remainder*

> "That's BackOffice Admin Portal.
> **Admin-only. Secure. Self-service. AI inside.**
> One front door for every system — and no more IT tickets for routine work.
> Happy to take questions."

---

## Presenter Notes

- **Pace.** ~10 minutes of talk + ~3 minutes of demo. Slides 2, 3 and 6 carry the story — don't rush them.
- **Audience-fit.**
  - *Executive room:* lean on slides 2, 3, 6, 12, 13. You can skip slides 7 and 11.
  - *Operations / admin team:* spend more time on slides 8, 9, 10 and the demo.
  - *IT / engineering:* add a beat on security (slide 2/4) and on how routine self-service removes their ticket queue.
- **Don't read the slides.** They are visual anchors; the story lives in the talk track.
- **Demo safety net.** If the live app misbehaves: have a screenshot fallback for the Dashboard, Dealers list, and the AI chat reply.
- **The big idea (memorise this).** *"One admin-only portal. Self-service for the work IT does today. AI inside. Built on operator feedback."*
